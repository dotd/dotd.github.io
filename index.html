About

AI that works in the real world

I am an AI expert specializing in Reinforcement Learning, Robotics, Time Series, and Computer Vision. My main interest is building autonomous systems — whether virtual or physical — across both high-tech and traditional industries.


Currently, I lead the Autonomous group at ForSight Robotics, where my team transforms human-operated surgical robots into autonomous ones. Previously, I founded and led the Bosch AI Research Lab in Israel, guiding 15 research scientists and engineers on projects spanning autonomous dozers, robotic manipulation, scheduling, and video prediction.


I serve as an Area Chair and reviewer at leading AI conferences including NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, and AISTATS, and have co-organized workshops at RSS and XAI.
